Mochi Ninja

Be the shadow. A cute top-down ninja stealth adventure.

Mochi, a tiny apprentice ninja, sneaks through lantern-lit villages, harbour docks, tea gardens, castles and a yokai forest to win back the treasures greedy Lord Gonzo stole. Snuff lanterns, run the rooftops, zip across on a kunai rope and bonk guards to sleep — nobody gets hurt.

  • 96 handcrafted levels in 8 worlds, plus daily and endless Shadow Trials
  • Light and shadow stealth: lanterns decide how far guards can see
  • Rooftops, kunai zip-lines, Shadow Strike and Shadow Dash
  • Rescue, sabotage, tail and duel missions, with chapter bosses
  • Outfits, gadgets and a daily fortune from the lucky cat
